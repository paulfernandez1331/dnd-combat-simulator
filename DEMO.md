# Fifteen-minute demo

Practice once with the heuristic before you depend on a live model call. Open the app with `python -m streamlit run app.py`.

1. **Bestiary (2 minutes).** Show the monster count, the spell count, and the exclusion table. Search for Fireball and say that a supported spell is chosen from Action or Bonus beside the current token and aimed on the mat. The 2024 record keeps the dice and puts the Dexterity save and the 20-foot sphere in the description. Search for Goblin Warrior and point at Nimble Escape: it is a bonus action, and the fight log will say the goblin takes Disengage.

2. **Encounter (3 minutes).** Click **Load four goblin warriors**. The party is a 5th-level fighter and wizard. The mat shows each name and its hit points. Party tokens are teal and enemy tokens are red. Click a token to select it, or an empty square to move it, then click **Start fight**. Mention **Load bugbear patrol** (a Bugbear Warrior and two Hobgoblin Warriors). You need both layouts later, because challenge rating does not vary inside one of them.

3. **One turn (3 minutes).** On the fighter's turn, open **Action**, then **Attack**. Longsword and Longbow are both listed before you move. Attack does not arm a weapon by itself, because there are two. Click **Longsword**. If no goblin is in reach, the mat says to move closer. Click a green square, open Attack again, click **Longsword**, and click a goblin. The action is still available for the second swing. Click **End turn** when you want to pass. The wizard's armor class is 15. On the wizard's turn, open **Action**, click **Fireball**, and point at a gold square among the goblins. The amber template follows the pointer. Click that square to cast. The log should show one Dexterity save and one damage roll per creature, and the 3rd-level slot should be spent. If a goblin is next, click **Use the heuristic instead** and let it move, attack, and end. If you have a key in `.env`, click **Let the model choose** once. The model is asked one action at a time, up to six choices on that turn. If a reply is unusable, that choice is logged as a fallback. Do not start a model batch during the demo.

4. **Batch (4 minutes).** Open **Simulate**. Set fights to 30 and enemy policy to heuristic. Start it, then click **Refresh progress** until it finishes. Go back to **Encounter**, click **Restore opening layout**, load the bugbear patrol, and run another 30 heuristic fights. Say that dice come from the seed. A model batch spends up to six calls on every enemy turn and would not repeat exactly. The warning on this page is that upper bound.

5. **Analysis (3 minutes).** Leave **Last batch only** on and show the batch you just ran: who took the first turn, who scored the first knockout, and how many turns each side took. If concentration never came up, the page says so. Turn the toggle off to pool both layouts and read the layout table. The rounds histogram and the outlier table are still there. `report/encounter-simulation-report.docx` is the older 2014 cohort, when one action ended the turn. Say that before you compare it with a batch you just ran. The toggle keeps those old fights out until you turn it off. If a key is configured, click **Write the report** and read the written summary.

Keep the sidebar rules expander for a question about what was left out. The short answer is cover, beast-form Wild Shape, and anything that only matters on a rest or a skill check. Placed zones and conjured tokens from the 2024 SRD stay up. Summons that need a missing stat block do not.
